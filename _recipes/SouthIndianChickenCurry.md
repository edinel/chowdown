ingredients:
- "# South Indian spice mix"
- "1 tbsp coriander seeds"
- "1 inch cinnamon (cassia) bark"
- "1/4 tsp black peppercorns"
- "1 clove"
- "2 green cardamom pods"
- "2 arms star anise (rip 2 arms off the star)"
- "1/2 tsp cumin seed"
- "1/2 tsp poppy seed"
- "Pinch ajwain (carom seed)"
- "1-2 whole dried red chilies (I used chile de arbol), seeds removed"
- "# Onion paste"
- "2 cups onions, coarsely chopped"
- "2 tbsp vegetable oil"
- "1 cup water"
- "# Curry"
- "2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3 pieces each (about 680 g)"
- "4 tbsp vegetable oil"
- "1 tbsp garlic ginger paste"
- "15&ndash;20 fresh curry leaves (optional)"
- "2&ndash;3 green chilies, coarsely chopped"
- "5 tsp South Indian spice mix (from above)"
- "1/2 tsp black pepper"
- "1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder"
- "1 tsp kosher salt"
- "1 small can tomato paste"
- "1 tsp tamarind paste"
- "1/2 cup coconut milk"

directions:
- "# South Indian spice mix"
- "Start with the coriander, black peppercorns, clove, cardamom pods, cinnamon, and 2 arms of star anise. Toast in a dry skillet until fragrant, then add the cumin seed, poppy seed, ajwain, and chilies. Cool completely and grind to a fine powder."
- "# Onion paste"
- "Microwave or sauté the chopped onions until soft. Blend with the oil and water until smooth.  (Don't forget the water, like I did the first time)"
- "# Curry"
- "In a small bowl, combine the 5 tsp spice mix with the black pepper, Kashmiri chili powder, and salt."
- "Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat until it shimmers. Add the green chilies and curry leaves and cook about 30 seconds."
- "Add the tomato paste and cook until it starts to darken."
- "Add the garlic ginger paste and cook until the paste is very dark and the ginger and garlic are cooked"
- "Lower the heat to medium-low. Add the combined ground spices and stir constantly until fragrant, 30&ndash;40 seconds. Do not let them burn."
- "Add the onion paste, raise the heat to medium-high, and cook 4&ndash;5 minutes, stirring occasionally and covering loosely to control splatter. The mixture will darken and may look dry."
- "Lower the heat to medium-low. Add the chicken and stir. Cover and simmer, stirring occasionally, for about 10 minutes.  The mixture will darken and start to stick. "
- "Stir in the coconut milk and tamarind paste and cook for 20-30 minutes until the chicken is tender and the gravy slightly reduced."
- "Garnish with additional diced green chilies. Serve with rice and parathas."

---

Adapted from the South Indian chicken curry recipe at [glebekitchen.com](https://glebekitchen.com/south-indian-chicken-curry/). I've tweaked the recipe mixing in a few Western techniques to brown more things; if I do it again I'll probably brown the chicken too to get some of that flavor.
