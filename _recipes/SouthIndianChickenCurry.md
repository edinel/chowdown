ingredients:
- "# South Indian spice mix"
- "1 tbsp coriander seeds"
- "1 inch cinnamon (cassia) bark"
- "1/4 tsp black peppercorns"
- "1 clove"
- "2 green cardamom pods"
- "2 arms star anise"
- "1/2 tsp cumin seed"
- "1/2 tsp poppy seed"
- "Pinch ajwain (carom seed)"
- "3 whole dried red chilies"
- "# Onion paste"
- "2 cups onions, coarsely chopped"
- "2 tbsp vegetable oil"
- "1 cup water"
- "# Curry"
- "6 boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3 pieces each (about 680 g)"
- "4 tbsp vegetable oil"
- "1 tbsp garlic ginger paste"
- "15&ndash;20 fresh curry leaves (optional)"
- "2&ndash;3 green chilies, coarsely chopped"
- "5 tsp South Indian spice mix (from above)"
- "1/2 tsp black pepper"
- "1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder"
- "1 tsp kosher salt"
- "2 tbsp tomato paste, diluted with water to a passata consistency"
- "1 tsp tamarind paste"
- "1/2 cup coconut milk"

directions:
- "# South Indian spice mix"
- "Toast all of the spice mix ingredients in a dry pan over medium heat until fragrant. Cool completely, then grind to a fine powder. This makes more than one batch of curry needs; store the extra in an airtight jar."
- "# Onion paste"
- "Microwave the chopped onions until soft. Blend with the oil and water until smooth."
- "# Curry"
- "In a small bowl, combine 5 tsp of the spice mix with the black pepper, Kashmiri chili powder, and salt."
- "Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat until it shimmers. Add the green chilies and curry leaves and cook about 30 seconds."
- "Add the garlic ginger paste and cook until the spluttering stops."
- "Lower the heat to medium-low. Add the combined ground spices and stir constantly until fragrant, 30&ndash;40 seconds. Do not let them burn."
- "Add the diluted tomato paste, stir to combine, raise the heat to medium, and cook about 1 minute."
- "Add the onion paste, raise the heat to medium-high, and cook 4&ndash;5 minutes, stirring occasionally and covering loosely to control splatter. The mixture will darken and may look dry."
- "Lower the heat to medium-low. Add the chicken and stir. Cover and simmer 5 minutes, then flip the pieces and continue cooking until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 170&deg;F, about 10&ndash;12 minutes total."
- "Stir in the coconut milk and tamarind paste and cook about 1 minute."
- "Garnish with additional diced green chilies. Serve with rice and parathas."

---

Adapted from the South Indian chicken curry recipe at [glebekitchen.com](https://glebekitchen.com/south-indian-chicken-curry/). Garlic ginger paste can be bought or made ahead; it keeps a few weeks in the fridge.
