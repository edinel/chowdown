ingredients:
- "# Rabbit and aromatics"
- "1 rabbit, hind legs boned and saddle halved crosswise (reserve the rib cage and front legs for stock)"
- "1&ndash;2 large carrots, chopped"
- "1&ndash;2 onions, chopped"
- "3 garlic cloves, unpeeled and crushed"
- "A small bunch of fresh thyme"
- "A sprig or two of rosemary"
- "25g (about 2 tbsp) butter"
- "2.5cl (about 1 1/2 tbsp) vegetable oil, clarified butter, or goose/duck fat"
- "Salt and pepper"
- "1dl (scant 1/2 cup) water or dry white wine, for deglazing"
- "1/4 liter (1 cup) brown poultry or veal stock, enriched by browning the reserved rabbit carcass"
- "# For the hay bed"
- "A generous quantity of food-safe, pesticide-free hay, thoroughly rinsed"
- "Straw, for covering"

directions:
- "# Prep"
- "Bone the hind legs and trim away any loose bone fragments. Halve the saddle crosswise. Reserve the rib cage and front legs for the stock."
- "Rinse the hay thoroughly in several changes of water."
- "# Searing"
- "Season the rabbit pieces with salt and pepper. Sear them, rounded side down first, in the oil, clarified butter, or goose/duck fat, browning on all sides without drying them out. Cook the legs and saddle halves in separate batches so the pan isn't crowded."
- "Remove the meat. In the same pan, sweat the carrots, onions, and garlic. Deglaze with the white wine or water, scraping up the browned bits."
- "# Hay-baking"
- "Make a bed of the rinsed hay in a heavy, ovenproof pot. Lay the legs and saddle halves on top."
- "Pour the deglazed pan juices and vegetables over the meat, then tuck in the rosemary and thyme."
- "Cover with a thick layer of straw, then foil, then the pot's lid, sealing it as airtight as possible."
- "Bake at 170&deg;C (340&deg;F): about 1 hour for the legs, 1 hour 15 minutes for the saddle halves. If cooking both in one pot, remove the legs after about an hour and return the saddle halves for roughly another 15 minutes."
- "# Sauce and serving"
- "Lift out the meat and discard the hay, straw, and vegetables. Deglaze the pot with a little water or wine, strain, and reduce."
- "Add the brown stock and reduce again to a light, flavorful jus."
- "Serve the rabbit with the jus spooned over."
